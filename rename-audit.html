🔄 Rename Audit

Audit known business renamings against your timeline data — find split visit counts from the same physical location

How it works: Click Detect Renames to automatically scan your data for locations where the same physical place has been recorded under multiple different names (e.g. a business that was rebranded). The changeover date will be estimated automatically — adjust it if you know the exact date. You can also add pairs manually. Then click Run Audit to see full details — visit counts, date ranges, anomaly detection (old name used after changeover), timeline visualisation, and the specific days each name appeared.
